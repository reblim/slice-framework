# Quick Start: one initiative, three slices, 20 minutes

**Apply SLICE to one real piece of work in five steps; if it takes more than 20 minutes, that is a finding we want.** Use the three [templates](/alpha/templates).

1. **Pick one roadmap item (3 minutes).** Something you plan to work on in the next two weeks. Classify it: a **Bet** if its value is uncertain, an **Obligation** if it is required, an **Improvement** if the benefit is clear and small. Fill the fields for that type in a Roadmap item.
2. **For a Bet, write the decision criterion (3 minutes).** One hypothesis, what you believe today and why, and a criterion you can actually count at your volume: "continue if at least 5 of the first 20 users do X" beats "conversion rises 2%". Set a review date.
3. **Cut three slices (5 minutes).** Each slice is the smallest piece someone can verify on its own. Give each a planning mode: 0 if obvious, 1 if bounded, E if a question about value or price blocks it, 2 for architecture, 3 for money, access or sensitive data.
4. **Fill one Slice card per slice (6 minutes).** Seven fields to fill: title, roadmap item, mode, size, work type, forecast and acceptance evidence. Leave the defaults (risk, AI leverage, evidence level F1, state Ready) unless they differ.
5. **Name your constraint and your weekly check (3 minutes).** Where does work wait longest today? Assume review if you don't know. Start no new slice while more than about a week of work waits there. Put a 20-minute weekly check in your calendar.

After each slice is Accepted, fill an Evidence record. Most of it should come from timestamps in your issue tracker or Git history.

**Do not** estimate in hours, multiply estimates by an "AI factor", or write percentiles before you have measured history.
